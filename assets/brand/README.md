# Zencelades logo

Masters for the Zencelades emblem and wordmark. Nothing on the site uses them yet.

The emblem is the piece itself: the sphere carrying a frame of Enceladus, lit by three
projectors whose beams form the triangle of the holder. A plume rises from the tiger
stripes at the pole and a seated figure is inside the sphere.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `zencelades-hero.png` | Emblem and wordmark on a star field, 2400 px wide. Headers and cards. |
| `zencelades-lockup.svg`, `.png` | Emblem and wordmark side by side, transparent, for dark grounds. |
| `zencelades-lockup-stacked.svg` | Emblem above wordmark, transparent, for dark grounds. |
| `zencelades-emblem.svg`, `.png` | Emblem alone, transparent, for dark grounds. |
| `zencelades-emblem-plain.svg` | The emblem without the seated figure. |
| `zencelades-icon.svg`, `.png` | Emblem on a rounded dark tile. Use this one on light grounds. |
| `zencelades-emblem-mono.svg`, `zencelades-wordmark-mono.svg` | One colour. They paint in `currentColor`, so they take the ink of whatever embeds them. |

The colour emblem needs a dark ground: the beams and glow are light on dark. On a light
ground use the icon tile or the one-colour emblem.

Colours: ground `#050d17`, ink `#edf4f4`, beams `#35e0ff`, `#ff4fa8`, `#ffc247`.

## The moon frame

The moon is one frame of catalog source E02 (`assets/enceladus-videos/catalog.json`),
"South-pole surface to Enceladus and Webb", at 5.7 seconds, when the whole globe is in
shot. It is cropped to the disc (centre 820, 736, radius 304 in the
1920x1080 frame), sharpened and graded towards ice blue, and embedded in each colour SVG.

Credit: NASA's Goddard Space Flight Center Conceptual Image Lab,
<https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/20384/>. The catalog records it as public domain under the
SVS policy, with the credit preserved. The frame is NASA's and anyone may use it; only
the arrangement around it is this project's. Using it does not imply NASA endorses the
project.
