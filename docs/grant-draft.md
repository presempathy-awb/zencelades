## The artwork

Enceladus Within connects the ocean around us, the ocean within a human body, and the hidden ocean of Saturn's moon Enceladus. A spherical image moves between a human presence, fractured ice, an imagined subsurface ocean and luminous plumes. Motion changes the transitions so the audience sees a world responding to people rather than a fixed film.

The long-term vision places a performer inside a purpose-designed sphere. The supplied revision 3 develops the optical system and two possible truck interfaces without an occupant. This application should promise only the exhibition scope that the artist can deliver and the event accepts.

## Why Love Burn

The proposed transition between a person and an ocean moon can support the 2027 Showtime! theme through live presence. A dry-land, externally performed version could let visitors see their own motion influence the moon imagery. This interaction is proposed, not demonstrated hardware. Maintain a calm moon sequence if live input is missing, and design an attractive experience when no performer is present.
