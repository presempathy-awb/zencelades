# Occupied soft spheres — prior work and useful design lessons

Research for P029–P036, September 30, 2026. The purpose is to improve Andrew's
DIY basket, not to substitute a commercial show purchase for the $3,000 cap.
These examples establish what was built or documented; none validates the
unidentified sphere, donor ring, webbing route or host for this project.

The Fishpipe patent drawing is the most directly useful public external-strap
illustration found. Its straps run to end rings, with adjustment accommodating
inflation changes. It does not show Andrew's three-leg top lift. Keep this
distinction in the grant drawings rather than calling the basket a proven copy.

TreePod's manual limits its own pod to 500 lb, requires low hanging height and
prohibits standing, jumping and swinging. Those are product-specific conditions,
not allowances for this sphere. The generic manual prohibits nonfixed/vehicle
anchors, while a separate [TreePod hitch product page](https://mytreepod.com/products/hitch-mount)
describes a stationary vehicle mount: model/revision compatibility would need
resolution before using that combination. The website also shows product
availability problems; no purchasable bargain or donated equipment is assumed.

## The small ring, swivel and halyard

A swivel accommodates rotation; it does not approve the rest of the lifting
system. For example, [Petzl's SWIVEL OPEN technical notice](https://www.petzl.com/sfc/servlet.shepherd/version/download/068Tx00000IoRiqIAF)
separates a 5 kN maximum working load from 23 kN breaking strength and requires
compatible connected components and inspection. This is a comparison, not the
selected swivel or a recommendation to load this basket to either number.
The complete lift, controlled lowering, host and person-retrieval arrangement
still require design. A halyard drawn in CAD supplies none of those details.

[SpanSet's lifting-sling directions](https://www.spanset.com/uploads/default/PIMTree/manualbook-lifting-id-ena3.pdf)
explain load distribution, sling-angle effects, contact protection and the
consequences of unequal legs. They concern their lifting products, not approval
for performer suspension. The practical lesson here is that soft webbing still
transmits substantial forces to its contact surface. Avoid representing three
equal-looking straps as three equally loaded straps under moving occupancy.

## Trampoline donor and local materials

The [six-foot trampoline manual](https://images.thdstatic.com/catalog/pdfImages/4b/4bfde4ff-839e-40bb-b52d-e19f43f49c8e.pdf)
documents a segmented frame in a specific ground-supported product. A salvaged
ring offers pre-curved stock, but its tube and joints must be assessed for the
new load path. Identify the actual donor before assuming its diameter, steel,
condition, joint retention or original load rating. Manufacturer literature
for one donor does not identify another.

[Altitude Steel](https://www.altitudesteel.com/carbon-supply) documents a remnant
area sold by weight. Compare known drops for the straight triangle and a rolled
ring with the work required to inspect and adapt a donor. No price-per-pound,
material list or quote has been obtained. Denhac's
[PrimeWeld TIG 225X page](https://denhac.org/wiki/primeweld-tig225x) is a documented
fabrication resource; Andrew has independently confirmed access and a bender.
