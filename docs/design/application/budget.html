Tier 1 cashOld phones inside the zorb running the project's own app: camera feed and mic feed to the laptop, looking and listening out through the clear skin (owned, or second-hand at Andrew's USD 25 budget)2025050allowanceAndrew's direction, October 1, 2026: USD 25 phones inside the zorb with a custom app that streams camera and mic to the laptop. Spoken trigger: nothing is projected until the person on the spot says 'I am the man on the moon' (or woman, or whatever they are); offline speech recognition on the laptop listens for the phrase through the phone mics, and the switch at the spot is the fallback trigger. Any Android or iPhone with a camera and Wi-Fi works; no service plan is needed for a local camera app, and the stream is composited live on the host and never stored. They go in before inflation on internal mounts with a USB power bank; the clear TPU skin is their window, which is why the projection medium is haze inside the zorb (Codex's P014/P015 research) with a frosted liner as the fallback, settled at the bench test. Fallback if no old phones turn up: a new Straight Talk Moto G Play 2026 at USD 49.88 (Walmart).
