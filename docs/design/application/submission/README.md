# Love Burn 2027 submission kit

Everything the Airtable form can take, in upload order. The answers for every
text field are in [`docs/grant-application-packet.md`](../../../grant-application-packet.md);
paste from there. The form is
https://airtable.com/appZEXWPCFtaKm6NA/pag1ytRqyhwaXdZTE/form and it times
out, so paste rather than compose in it.

## What the form accepts

| Field | Accepts | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ART PROJECT PHOTOS | JPG or PNG ("drawings, photos, napkin sketches") | under 5 MB each, under 20 MB in all |
| BUDGET SPREADSHEET | PDF, XLS or CSV only; printable PDF preferred | one proper budget, or it is not considered |
| Video Proposal | a link (Google Drive, iCloud, any public link) | under two minutes |
| Art Description (Not for Website) | text; a public photo or video drive link goes in here | none |

Nothing else uploads: every drawing goes in as an image, every document as a link.

## BUDGET SPREADSHEET

`budget.pdf` (printable, the form's preference). `budget.xlsx` is the same budget as a
spreadsheet with live formulas for the line totals, subtotals, 20% contingency and total,
and `budget.csv` is the flat data. The PDF and CSV are generated by `just grant-ledger` from
`assets/grant-build-bom.json`; the xlsx was built from the generated ledger JSON.

## Before submitting, Andrew fills

- Name, phone, email, artist name, camp, social handles, bought-tickets, insurance, excitement line.
- The rig's model, height and rated load from its manual; the zorb's exact diameter, weight and tunnel size.
- Amount already invested; total estimated investment (ledger high $5,242 plus owned gear at replacement value plus travel).
- The video (90-second script in the packet), uploaded to Google Drive as anyone-with-link; the link goes in the Video field.
- A Google Drive folder with these images and the packet, link pasted in the committee description.
- Whether the GitHub mirror is public, so both repository links open.

Every bracketed item in the packet is one of these; search for `[` before pasting.
