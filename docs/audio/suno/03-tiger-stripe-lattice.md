## Suno controls

Custom mode; Instrumental on; Lyrics empty. Choose an available model and record
its exact version. Initial audition settings: Weirdness 40%, Style Influence
80%. These are proposed comparison baselines, not measured optima.
Audio Influence is unused without an audio reference. Aim for 3–4 minutes in
editing; exact duration, tempo, key, phrase lengths and loop seams are not guaranteed.

## Style prompt — copy this field

```text
Instrumental crystalline IDM and downtempo, 96 BPM, D minor, steady 4/4. Interlocking glass mallets, dry syncopated clicks, restrained broken-beat drums, warm sub bass and slowly shifting translucent synth chords. Precise repeating patterns suggest blue fissures spreading across an icy moon, with a curious three-note rising motif appearing between the percussion. Intricate but breathable, luminous rather than threatening. Begin with sparse percussion, unfold in clear repeating phrases, open into a beatless middle, then rebuild and leave a clean percussion-and-pad outro.
```

## Exclude — separate field

```text
vocals, spoken words, harsh crackles, piercing bells, chaotic tempo changes, horror drones
```

## Production and acceptance

Use separate low-gain ice-crack effects; do not bake loud fracture effects into the music master.

Audition a baseline before increasing settings. Keep source song ID, model,
prompt revision, settings and the selected take together. Measure actual tempo,
key, integrated loudness and true peak; mark clean mix-in/mix-out bars after
listening. Preserve the original download and make runtime derivatives separately.
A reference to the shared motif is direction, not a guarantee that independently
generated songs will share the same melody. If that continuity matters, use an
approved original audio reference or arrange the motif in post-production.
