**P1 and P2 are valid final artistic configurations, not merely incomplete P3.**

side. An optional portrait overlay can use the same output as moon imagery;
head count follows the projected area, not the number of imagery layers.

Choose support mode and sphere size independently of optical count. Removing
one or two heads from a suspended layout changes balance; the remaining arms,
covers, cables and any required balancing hardware need their own layout check.
Do not put a head or stand into the central entrance just to centre the image.

### Reconcile with the actual $3,000 cap

The current nine-line allocation includes $150 lighting/control and $600 reserve.
Removing only the $150 lighting line leaves **$2,250 of other allocations**.
Retaining the $600 reserve leaves **$150 for the entire projection package**.
Thus even one new LS711HD at the workbook price makes the planning total

Fewer heads reduce cost, but they do not by themselves close this particular

documented. A second can be added only if the full event budget still fits.
No loan, rental rate or used bargain has yet been established. If other costs
drop through confirmed ownership or donated materials, record those reductions
before reallocating them; do not spend the same reserve twice.

## Optics screening, scaled to the current sphere studies

The workbook's flat-frame screen uses a landscape image tall enough to enclose
the sphere: width = diameter × aspect ratio, diagonal = diameter ×
sqrt(1 + aspect ratio²). For 16:9, a 2.5 m sphere requires a flat enclosing
rectangle about **4.44 m wide / 201 in diagonal**; 3.0 m requires **5.33 m /
241 in**. Those are frame dimensions, not measured illuminated spherical area.

Using the workbook's 0.496 throw ratio for ZH350ST/LS711HD, the corresponding
flat-reference lens distances are **2.20 m and 2.65 m**. That does not prove
focus on the curved skin, useful brightness, lack of spill, or fit on the arms.
It also does not use the arm length or boom reach as optical distance. A P1/P2
design may intentionally use a smaller patch instead of enclosing the whole
sphere height, changing the optical requirement.

For the next empty-surface trial, establish the selected head's actual lens
position, visible patch, curved focus, contrast and masking, then record the
participant-view and external-view result. Whole-projector weather protection,
power and payload remain separate from lens/brightness specifications.
