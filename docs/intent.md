## Purpose and people

Develop an interactive artwork connecting a human sense of embodiment with
Enceladus's ice, ocean and plumes, while retaining an accurate working record
of the concept, assets, alternatives, costs, permissions and evidence.
The present project name is provisional; the directory/service identity remains
`thatsnozorb` until a deliberate rename and migration are selected.

Andrew directs creative scope and submissions. Collaborators need original
assets, source provenance and bounded build tasks. Grant reviewers need a
coherent proposed experience, credible costs and an honest account of unknowns.
Operators need installation, weather, power, recovery and removal procedures.
Visitors need an understandable, accessible experience that does not require
being filmed or entering a sphere.

The website supports those decisions. It is not the physical installation,
a structural certification, an award notice or a complete shared crew application.

## Smallest useful next slices

The immediate software/storage slice is the scoped research publisher and
immutable four-file readback, preserving the original receipts. The next
physical learning slice is a dry, stationary, unoccupied material/projection
comparison without internal cameras. This is a recommendation consistent with
v3's development scope, not Andrew's final full-scale layout selection.

The larger occupied, moving, truck-supported water concept remains the artistic
vision to evaluate. Starting with that whole configuration would combine optical,
structural, environmental, occupancy and permission uncertainty in one test.
A dry prototype can still express a complete moon/ocean experience while those
questions receive separate evidence.
