Status: common holder retained and tested. P041 replaces the platform fallback
with detachable lander legs; the original prompt below is historical.

Andrew's prompt:

supports under the triangle for the ground fallback; do not replace the holder

sphere, contacts, triangle interfaces and optional camera attachments common.
