# P004: Grant guides and relevant funded-art precedents

Actor: Codex. Status: complete for research/publication. Captured September 30, 2026.

Andrew's request, verbatim:

> gather loveburn like art submission guides burning man great art grants that got big especially similar ones, guides to great art grants.

1. Gather current official Love Burn, Burning Man and relevant regional-burn
   application/preparation guidance, with the applicable year, stage and limits.
   Do not transfer another program's award cap or process to Love Burn.
2. Find close precedents in spherical/astronomical, projection/light, immersive,
   participatory and suspended art. Verify funded status independently from an
   installation listing. Where work later toured, entered a museum or became a
   civic installation, document that separate outcome and any disclosed funding.
3. Build a curated research library with primary links, concise case studies,
   confidence labels, transferable lessons and pitfalls for Enceladus. No
   invented grant amounts, causal claims about why juries chose work, acceptance
   odds or engineering approval.
4. Translate the evidence into a practical proposal guide: artistic premise,
   participant journey, visuals/prototype, crew, feasible scope, cash/in-kind
   budget, schedule, site/power/accessibility/LNT and the official-offer boundary.
   Separate published requirements from Codex's recommendations.
5. Save the library and source register in the project, link from the grant
   guidance, publish through the existing authorized site recipe, verify the
   artifacts and update P004. No application, outreach, purchase or submission.

Complete when the curated guides and evidenced precedents are usable locally
and on the tailnet site, and the proposal recommendations trace to them. Existing
pytest/browser-consent and PG18/Pawthentik gates remain unchanged.

Delivered: `/grants/`, the complete annotated library, proposal workshop and
28-source register. Twelve guide entries and eight cases distinguish six
confirmed funded works from two form comparisons. No individual award amount
or private jury reasoning is asserted. [Publication evidence](../grant-library-verification.md)
