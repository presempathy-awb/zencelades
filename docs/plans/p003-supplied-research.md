Biggest Love Burn art gotcha: **nothing counts until an official Art Committee Offer Notice email.** Organizer chats, verbal yeses, placement talk — none of it binds grant $, size, placement, or approval. You must apply on the Airtable form (`theloveburn.com/art`).

Apps are **placement and/or grant**. They’re much less selective than BM honoraria (e.g. 2026: 326+ proposals, 226+ granted). Grants can include cash plus in-kind (power, propane, heavy equipment, tickets, labor) — exact $ tiers aren’t published. Early bird was July 31; final close is **today, Sept 30** (date only, no clock time).

Other gotchas that bite people:

- If you accept and can’t deliver: **return tickets or pay back funded grants**
- Don’t wait for artist codes — they may come as late as October; buy tickets yourself (same price as general for 2027)
- Register before you show up with art (“tell us first”)
- Artist owns design, transport, install, daily upkeep, and full removal / LNT
- Beach reality: Virginia Key sand + wind (big stakes), limited shared generator power (labeled outdoor cords; no RV hookups), public park rules
- Early-entry dates on the site conflict / some pages are still 2023 — confirm after your Offer Notice

Vs Burning Man: Love Burn is **not affiliated with BMP as of 2024**. No LOI → invite-only full proposal pipeline; one Airtable app instead. Hundreds of projects get placed/granted on a 4-day beach weekend, not a tiny honoraria subset of a desert city. DTCs are theme-camp tickets, not art grants. Questions: `art@theloveburn.com` / `tickets@theloveburn.com`.
