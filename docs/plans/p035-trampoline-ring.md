# P035 — Six-foot trampoline ring as a donor

Andrew's prompt:

> maybe  6 ft trampoline ring

Research a reclaimed six-foot trampoline frame as a candidate circular seating
loop. Check the nominal diameter against the triangle geometry. Donor steel,
wall, corrosion, holes, sectional joints and their retention must be identified;
a trampoline's original ground-use rating is not a suspended basket rating.
Do not assign a free or cheap purchase price without an actual donor or quote.
Compare with known steel remnants and a purpose-rolled ring; labor is in-kind.
