Love Burn 2027 · SHOWTIME! You are not the audience. You are part of the show.

You are the man
on the moon.

Enceladus is a small moon of Saturn with a whole ocean hidden under its ice. Most of a human body is water too. Zencelades is a glowing moon, eight feet across, that you can climb inside. Step through its tunnel, sit on the soft floor, and say the words: “I am the man on the moon” (or woman, or whatever you are). Only then does the moon take you: your own face, blown up to the size of the moon, appears across its surface, fading in beneath the ice. The ice cracks. The hidden ocean floods through. Plumes rise from where you sit, and then, slowly, the moon takes itself back.

How it works.

  1. Climb in. A tunnel at knee height, a soft floor, a crew member at the door. One person at a time. The blower runs the whole time and the tunnel is never closed.
  2. Say the words. A switch at the door turns the cameras on when you go in and off when you leave. Nothing is projected until you say “I am the man on the moon”. A stop switch at the seat ends it whenever you like.
  3. Become the moon. Two small cameras inside see only you, and only while you are there; nothing is recorded. Your face fades in under the ice, the ice cracks, the ocean floods through, plumes rise.
  4. The moon takes itself back. Say the words again, or climb out, and it returns to a calm, breathing loop of ice and water for the next person. Everyone outside gets the best seat: watching a friend become the man on the moon.

Two 3,600-lumen projectors paint the sphere, a little haze in the air outside makes the beams visible, and a quiet soundscape carries the story. By day it is a plain white moon on the sand. After dark it is the show, and so are you.
