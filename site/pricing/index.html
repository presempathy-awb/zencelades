Surface, supports, engineering, media, labor, permits and insurance are allowances. Replace them with supplier and specialist quotes. Projector hire is calculated separately from the observed daily rate. A low-high range is not a confidence interval.

Where the money changes.

Water → dry land. Removes marine placement, splash/wave and recovery work from this scope. Outdoor rain, wind, electrical protection and site permissions still matter.

Four projectors → two or one. At the current $495/day anchor and six days, each projector accounts for $2,970 before contingency. Fewer projectors means a deliberately narrower viewing experience.

No internal cameras. The original four-camera BOM line is $500–$1,600. Removing capture also simplifies synchronization, but that additional labor saving has not been measured.

Fixed does not automatically mean cheap.

A sphere supported over a truck bed avoids a long cantilever. A fixed arm extending beyond the truck still needs custom attachments, stabilization and structural review. Existing hitch ratings do not approve this geometry.

Hanging needs an actual approved point.

The host-point option carries an access allowance. It is conditional on owner and event approval, the complete load path and a wind plan. If no appropriate structure exists, use the independent gantry price.

Start with an optical bench.

The original P0 allowance is $1,875–$5,750 including contingency. That is a separate early prototype, not a complete event installation. Use it to choose the material and verify projection before committing to a full sphere.

Prices with their context.

The September 30 study recorded Miami Sound Rental at $495 per 24 hours for a 6,000-lumen projector in an IP65 truss enclosure, with delivery/setup from $240 per order. These historical rental inputs are not current quotes or the main proposal’s purchase prices.

Resolume Arena is listed at €799 for one computer. This is a euro price; the USD media/software rows remain allowances, with no exchange rate or tax asserted.

The remaining ranges are new concept allowances informed by the supplied v3 BOM. Its combined P0 + P1 estimate was $40,875–$111,500 including contingency; these alternatives change the experience and cannot be treated as vendor savings against an identical build.
