Turn references into
our own proposal.

  1. Lead with the human/ocean connection and a clear visitor journey.
  2. Choose one complete dry-land base scope and a costed fallback from the pricing alternatives.
  3. Label concept art, prior work and physical tests accurately. Disclose generated imagery where requested.
  4. Name the team, milestones, requested resources and unresolved approvals.
  5. Check reviewer access to every attachment. A private site link alone is insufficient.
