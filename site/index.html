A world shaped by presence.

The artistic ambition is a coherent spherical image: a person fading into the ice, ocean and plumes of Saturn’s moon. Human motion and the surrounding environment would influence the transitions.

The current v3 package develops an unoccupied optical and geometry prototype. Live spherical reconstruction, actual projector calibration and a purpose-designed performer enclosure remain future work.
