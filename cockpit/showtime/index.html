About the installation & videos

Projection & source notes

Ground lander and Aerial rig share the same projected round zorb; switching support keeps the flight, camera or moving video, orbit and fullscreen running. The aerial version includes the suspended basket, webbing, collector and surrounding support frame. Moon and human imagery stay on the zorb wall. The projected shell is translucent, with softer light at its curved edges and clear spacer ties inside. Stronger haze rises from the ground unit beneath the zorb and catches the beams in both versions. The separate Internal haze study control shows a proposed feed and illuminated vapor inside the sphere; this does not establish improved image clarity or safe occupied operation. Machine/fluid compatibility, ventilation and exposure must be assessed before any occupied trial; use Hazer on/off to remove or restore the vapor and air beams without changing the Haze strength.

One Cassini global map wraps around the left, rear and right of the round inflatable zorb. The clear front and inner chamber remain visible, with less illumination on the top and a hazer below. Both is selected by default, with the person at full strength and Fade in & out off. Brightness starts at full strength and is separate from Effects. Moon shows the lunar map and film without the person; Body fills the source with the portrait or local video and the moving avatar without lunar imagery; Both merges them. Movie blend adjusts the lunar map/film mix in Moon and Both; Body bypasses the lunar map. A seated 3D portrait of Andrew stays inside both versions, with a shirtless body and navy shorts. Random motion shuffles settle, look, reach, stretch, recline and tucked crouch routines; Controls mode uses the sliders when no local input is active and senses camera/video movement when one is active. Movement response adjusts how much that movement stirs the body, moon blend and haze. Reduced motion holds the animated pose. The spherical zorb keeps its shape. Moon hides the person projection and avatar. In Body and Both, Reference portrait off with no live input hides the avatar. This is a concept likeness, not pose tracking or a body scan. A dark camera backdrop helps your face and body merge cleanly. This is a visual preview; projector output and haze still need calibration on the real installation. The ocean colors and lander landing are imagined.
