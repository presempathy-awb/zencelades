Projection & source notes

The sphere is an artistic preview of the wrap, not calibrated projector output or a 3D body scan. Camera backgrounds blend with you; a dark backdrop helps. Moon, lander, surface and ocean scenes mix original Babylon.js art with NASA Goddard visualizations, Cassini observations (NASA/JPL-Caltech/SSI) and Webb artwork (NASA/ESA/CSA, Leah Hustak/STScI). The ocean colors and lander landing are imagined. Film controls & full credits.
